How to play Tridoku

Use the same four rules in both modes:

  1. Each bold nine-cell region contains 1–9 once.
  2. Each outer side contains no repeated digit.
  3. Each of the three nine-cell legs of the shaded inner triangle contains 1–9 once.
  4. Matching digits cannot touch at an edge or a corner.

In Classic, fill the empty board. In Quick Solve, fill only the two marked targets, using the rest of the board as context. Free hints are available in both modes.

Achievements

First solves count with or without hints. The separate no-hints achievements use the in-game hint history in saved results; unsaved interruptions and outside help may not be reflected. Separate practice is not tracked as a hint on an attempt. Ordinary and Daily solves count, even when a Daily finish is practice. Achievements stay in this browser.
